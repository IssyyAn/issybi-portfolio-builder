Data and metric definitions
Illustrative / synthetic data — not real findings. One record per month, region and available category, January–June 2026. All amounts are synthetic USD revenue with no missing values. Total revenue is the sum of selected records; the average divides that total by the six reporting months. Leading category is the highest selected total, with alphabetical tie breaking. Every filter affects all metrics, charts and records. South + Furniture deliberately has no records. Reset restores all regions and categories. This local prototype has no live data connection, targets, authentication or native Power BI model.
